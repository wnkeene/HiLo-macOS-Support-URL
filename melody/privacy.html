Privacy Policy

Melody Time

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Simple Summary: Melody Time does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. Playback and catalog access use Apple Music through Apple’s MusicKit. Your Melody Time playlists may sync through your private iCloud account. We do not run developer user accounts.

Overview

Melody Time is an uncluttered music listening app for iPhone. It lets you browse genres, search the catalog, use your library, and manage your own Melody Time playlists. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking. Specifically, Melody Time does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Third-party analytics services
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • Developer-run user accounts or sign-in of our own

Apple Music and MusicKit

Catalog browsing, search, library access, and playback use Apple’s MusicKit and Apple Music. When you use those features, you sign in with your Apple Account and interact with Apple’s services according to Apple’s privacy practices. Catalog playback requires an Apple Music subscription.

Requests go from your device to Apple. We do not receive your Apple Music listening history as a developer analytics feed.

Local Storage and Private iCloud Sync

Melody Time stores app-owned data using Apple’s frameworks (including SwiftData). Depending on your device and iCloud availability, Melody Time playlists and related metadata may remain on the device or sync through your private iCloud database using CloudKit.

This stored data can include:

  • Melody Time playlist names and song references you add
  • Genre visibility preferences
  • App preferences stored via system UserDefaults

Private CloudKit data is associated with your Apple Account and is not shared publicly. We do not use it for profiling or advertising.

No Analytics or Tracking

Melody Time does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking technologies. The app’s privacy manifest declares that tracking is disabled.

No Account Required (Beyond Apple)

You do not create a Melody Time account with us. Using Apple Music features requires your Apple Account and, for catalog playback, an Apple Music subscription — managed by Apple.

Data Security

Network requests used by Melody Time rely on Apple’s platforms (MusicKit, iCloud/CloudKit) over HTTPS and Apple’s account systems.

Your Control

  • Playlists: You can edit or delete Melody Time playlists in the app.
  • Local app data: Removing the app removes its local data from that device.
  • iCloud data: If iCloud sync is enabled, related private app data is tied to your Apple Account and managed through Apple’s iCloud systems.
  • Apple Music: Subscription, library, and account settings are controlled in Apple’s Settings / Music apps.

Children's Privacy

Melody Time does not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own purposes. Because the app does not use advertising, analytics, or developer-run accounts, it is designed to minimize data collection beyond what Apple’s Music and iCloud services require for the features you use.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.
